# Moderator Guidance: Evaluation Criteria

## Key Factors to Weigh

1. **Technical Feasibility** (30%)
   - Can the solution meet the 1M records/second requirement?
   - Is the architecture sound?

2. **Timeline Risk** (25%)
   - Can the team deliver within 6 months?
   - What is the risk of delays?

3. **Maintainability** (25%)
   - Can the system be maintained for 5+ years?
   - Is the codebase accessible to the team?

4. **Total Cost of Ownership** (20%)
   - Development costs
   - Infrastructure costs
   - Training costs

## Red Flags to Watch For
- Ignoring the performance requirement
- Underestimating learning curves
- Overlooking hybrid solutions
- Cherry-picking benchmarks without context
